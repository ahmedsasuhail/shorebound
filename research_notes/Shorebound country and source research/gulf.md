# Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar): visa routes, salaries, job sources for Indian senior SWEs

Scope was reduced mid-task to AE, SA and QA. Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman were partly researched but no files were written for them. The research was done on 2026-11-22. Several official sites (mohre.gov.ae, saudieng.sa, sapr.gov.sa, mol.gov.qa) timed out or refused automated access, so many facts rest on law-firm or news summaries.

## Which work-visa routes apply, and their thresholds?

### Takeaway
Employer sponsorship is the norm in all three countries. The UAE also has two routes that don't depend on the employer: the Green Visa (AED 15,000 a month) and the Golden Visa (AED 30,000 a month). Saudi Arabia's high-skilled tier (since August 2025) has no official published wage figure. Qatar's long-term residency is open only to executives.

### Cited Findings
- UAE Green Visa (skilled): requires a salary of at least AED 15,000, MOHRE skill level 1–3, at least a bachelor's degree, and a MOHRE skilled permit or contract. The fee is AED 200 + 5% VAT, and GDRFA expects completion in 48 hours — [GDRFA](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/node/13903)
- UAE Golden Visa (skilled professional): requires MOHRE level 1 or 2 (IT counts under level 2), an attested bachelor's degree, and AED 30,000 a month backed by six months of bank statements — [ADDED Abu Dhabi](https://www.added.gov.ae/en/live/long-term-residency/abu-dhabi-golden-visa/for-specialists/for-skilled-professionals)
- In Dubai, the AED 30,000 must be basic salary, excluding allowances, and the applicant needs more than 2 years with the current employer (alert of 2026-06-12) — [Vialto](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/united-arab-emirates-immigration-further-developments-to-uae-golden-visa-eligibility-criteria)
- Emiratisation: firms with 50+ staff must grow Emirati skilled jobs by 2 points a year, up to 10% by 2026. Firms with 20–49 staff in 14 activities (including ICT) also have targets. The H1 2026 deadline was 30 June — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c03cqiu-june-deadline-for-achieving-emiratisation-targets), [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/uae-emiratisation-targets-expanded-to-smaller-establishments)
- Saudi skill-based permits: three tiers (high-skilled, skilled, basic). New hires are classified from 2025-08-03. High-skilled covers occupation groups 1–3 and needs a minimum wage, professional accreditation and a points score. Fragomen says the wage levels were "still to be determined" — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/saudi-arabia-new-skill-based-work-permit-classification-system-introduced.html). Centuro gives SAR 15,000 and ≥70 points, which is unverified and contradicted by other firms — [Centuro](https://www.centuroglobal.com/article/saudi-arabia-work-visa/)
- Saudi Premium Residency, Special Talent: healthcare and science professionals need SAR 35,000 a month, a bachelor's degree, 3+ years' experience and a whitelisted employer. Researchers need SAR 14,000 and executives SAR 80,000 — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/Saudi-Arabia-five-new-premium-residency-permit-categories-introduced.html). 3,484 private-sector entities have been whitelisted — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/gcc/saudi-3-484-private-sector-entities-approved-for-special-talent-premium-residency-yubq0slr)
- Saudi levies: aggregator sites report SAR 800 a month per expat where expats outnumber Saudis (SAR 700 otherwise) and SAR 400 a month per dependent. These are unofficial figures — [levy guide](https://hijri-calendars.com/blog/saudi-work-permit-levy-guide-2026?lang=en). MHRSD raised Saudization rates for engineering and procurement roles on 2026-01-04 — [EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0167-saudi-arabia-announces-increase-in-saudization-rates-for-roles-in-the-engineering-and-procurement-sectors)
- Qatar: the employer gets a Ministry of Labour recruitment approval, then an MOI work visa. The candidate completes biometrics, medicals and contract signing at a Qatar Visa Center in India. Sources disagree on the fee: QAR 300 in one, QAR 200 in another — [legal guide](https://commoner-law.com/qatar/immigration-pathways/work-visas), [Y-Axis](https://www.y-axis.com/blog/how-to-apply-qatar-work-visa-from-india/). MoL Decision 61 of 2026 (gazetted 2026-10-25) tightens licensing of recruitment offices — [report](https://www.legit.ng/people/1740021-qatar-releases-16-conditions-recruiting-foreigners-rules/)
- Qatar 10-year residency: QAR 50,000 a month for C-suite roles and QAR 80,000 for other executive directors — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/qatar-long-term-residence-permit-for-executives-and-entrepreneurs-announced.html)

### Inferences
- For a senior engineer who earns at least AED 15,000 and is classified at MOHRE level 1–3, the UAE Green Visa is the most reachable route that doesn't depend on the employer.
- Saudi employer levies probably make employers more selective about foreign hires.

### Gaps
- Official processing times and fees for the standard UAE and Saudi work visas.
- Whether Saudization applies to IT roles.
- Qatarisation in the private sector.
- Whether Dubai's basic-salary rule applies UAE-wide.

## How is a 3-year Indian bachelor's degree treated?

### Takeaway
No source states how a 3-year degree is treated. The main risk is Saudi Arabia's "Engineer" title.

### Cited Findings
- The Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE) checks whether an applicant's programme qualifies for the engineer title. It also ties IT iqama professions ("Software Development Specialist", "Software Engineering Specialist") to SCE registration — [SCE](https://www.saudieng.sa/English/Eservices/Accreditation), [SCE technicians](https://saudieng.sa/English/Eservices/Accreditation/Pages/Guidelines-for-Registration-of-Technicians.aspx)
- A forum report says SCE expects an 8-semester degree. This is anecdotal — [expat.com](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/872075-list-of-professions-which-need-registration-in-sce.html)
- India is covered by Saudi Professional Verification, which has been expanded to 160 countries — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-expands-professional-verification-programme-to-160-countries-1.500019969)
- The attestation chain reported for Qatar is: notary → state HRD → MEA → Qatar Embassy → MoFA Qatar. The source is a consultancy — [Y-Axis](https://www.y-axis.com/blog/how-to-apply-qatar-work-visa-from-india/)

### Inferences
- A BCA holder should agree a non-engineer visa title (developer, programmer or specialist) with the employer before the visa is issued, especially in Saudi Arabia.

### Gaps
- No official statement in any of the three countries on whether a 3-year bachelor's counts as "bachelor's or equivalent".

## Employer registers

### Takeaway
None of the three countries publishes a sponsor register. In the UAE, the app can only link users to the company-licence lookup and the MOHRE offer check.

### Cited Findings
- The UAE National Economic Register is linked from scam guidance (ner.economy.ae redirects to growth.gov.ae). MOHRE job offers carry a serial number and barcode that users can verify on the MOHRE site or app, or by calling 600590000 — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/high-paying-uae-job-offer-mohre-warns-job-seekers-about-recruitment-scams-1.500634931)
- Saudi Arabia's Jadarat platform is for Saudi nationals only — [HRDF](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/en/products-and-services/programs/individuals/other/jadarat/)

### Gaps
- Whether MOHRE's establishment classification lists are published.

## Job sites and access (robots.txt read 2026-11-22)

### Cited Findings
- Bayt: `User-agent: *` blocks /en/jobs/? and /en/jobs/*-jobs/, so only link-out is possible — [robots](https://www.bayt.com/robots.txt)
- GulfTalent: `Allow: /` except /jobs/search and /api/jobs/search. It sets ai-train=no, and ClaudeBot is blocked — [robots](https://www.gulftalent.com/robots.txt)
- Indeed: /rss, /*?rss and /m/viewjob are disallowed — [robots](https://ae.indeed.com/robots.txt)
- Akhtaboot and OpenSooq: `Disallow: /` for generic agents — [Akhtaboot](https://www.akhtaboot.com/robots.txt)
- DrJob: allows all agents and publishes job sitemaps — [robots](https://www.drjobpro.com/robots.txt)
- Qatar Living: allows everything except /api/ — [robots](https://www.qatarliving.com/robots.txt)
- Tanqeeb: blocks keyword and country query strings, but has a sitemap — [robots](https://www.tanqeeb.com/robots.txt)
- Dubizzle: doesn't block job paths, only /api/ and account paths — [robots](https://www.dubizzle.com/robots.txt)
- Naukrigulf and Laimoon: robots.txt could not be retrieved.

### Gaps
- No site's terms of service were reviewed.
- No free APIs were found.
- Reputation ratings are judgement calls, not backed by a survey.

## Salaries (senior SWE)

### Takeaway
Salary sources disagree widely, so confidence is low. Gulf offers are usually basic pay plus allowances, and sources don't always say which they quote.

### Cited Findings
- UAE: Indeed average for senior software engineers is AED 9,457 a month (job-posting based) — [Indeed](https://ae.indeed.com/career/software-engineer/salaries). PayScale average is AED 223,924 a year — [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/research/AE/Job=Senior_Software_Engineer/Salary). Jooble is around AED 28,451 a month — [Jooble](https://ae.jooble.org/salary/senior-software-engineer)
- Saudi Arabia: Levels.fyi Riyadh senior total compensation is a median of SAR 343,798, in a range of SAR 300,085–420,232 a year — [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/levels/senior/locations/riyadh-sau). Jooble senior developer is around SAR 19,877 a month — [Jooble](https://sa.jooble.org/salary/senior-developer/Riyadh)
- Qatar: Naukrigulf average is QAR 12,559 a month — [Naukrigulf](https://www.naukrigulf.com/salaries/senior-software-engineer-salary-in-qatar). Qatar Living gives QAR 26,000–40,000 a month for senior or lead engineers — [Qatar Living](https://www.qatarliving.com/en/article/salary-expectations-job-role-qatar)
- No Cooper Fitch or Hays technology figures could be read.

### Gaps
- Typical relocation packages: housing, schooling and flights.

## Scams

### Cited Findings
- MOHRE says real employers pay all recruitment costs, a visit visa does not allow work, and job seekers should verify the offer's barcode — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/high-paying-uae-job-offer-mohre-warns-job-seekers-about-recruitment-scams-1.500634931)
- MEA warns about unregistered agents overcharging by Rs 2–5 lakh. A genuine offer has a contract signed by the employer, the agent and the worker, and is on an employment visa. Agents can be checked on the eMigrate active RA list — [MEA advisory](https://www.hcigaborone.gov.in/docs/1703063160Advisory%20on%20overcharging%20by%20agents%20for%20overseas%20recruitment.pdf), [eMigrate RA list](https://emigrate.gov.in/ext/raList.action)
- Agents have misused employers' eMigrate logins to issue fake offers — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/going-out/society/agents-misuse-uae-employers-login-details-to-issue-fake-job-offers-1.1878795)

### Inferences
- Red flags the app can detect: fee or deposit wording, free-mail recruiter domains, contact only by WhatsApp, offers on a visit visa, and "free visa" wording (فيزا حرة).
